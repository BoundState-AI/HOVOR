# HOVOR! Native Slovak Review Packet — content `ancestry-slice-0.2.0`

Ahoj Tomáš! HOVOR! is an app that teaches Slovak to English speakers by
making them *speak* it. Every Slovak string below was machine-authored and
needs one pass from a native speaker before real learners see it. There are
two parts — you can do them in either order:

- **Part A (~2–3 hours):** read the content, mark what's wrong or unnatural.
- **Part B (~20 minutes):** record six short sentences on your phone.

## How to mark Part A

For each line, one of three verdicts:

- **OK** — leave it, or just tick it.
- **WRONG** — grammar/spelling/diacritics error → write the correction.
- **UNNATURAL** — grammatically fine but nobody would say it → write what a
  real person would say instead.

Also flag any **register** mismatches (formal where informal belongs, or vice
versa). Diacritics matter a lot here — the app grades learners on them, so a
missing mäkčeň in our content becomes a bug in someone's brain.

Reply in any form you like: annotated copy of this file, a list of line
numbers + fixes, voice memo walking through problems — whatever is fastest.

## Part A3 — Error-repair items

The app shows learners a *broken* sentence to fix. Please check that (a) the broken version is a mistake an English speaker would actually make, (b) the fixed version(s) are correct, (c) the explanation is true.

- `er_volam_sa_je`: ✗ Volám sa je Ján. → **volám sa ján**
  - explanation shown: „volám sa“ already contains the verb — no extra „je“. English speakers add it because of „my name IS“.
- `er_z_amerika`: ✗ Som z Amerika. → **som z ameriky**
  - explanation shown: „z“ changes the noun's ending: Amerika → z Ameriky. The noun's role in the sentence changed — watch the pattern.
- `er_sa_position`: ✗ Môj starý otec narodil sa v Píle. → **môj starý otec sa narodil v píle**
  - explanation shown: „sa“ wants second position in the clause: „...otec SA narodil...“, not „...narodil sa...“ after the subject phrase.
- `er_jednu_kavu`: ✗ Prosím si jeden káva. → **prosím si jednu kávu**
  - explanation shown: Ordering puts the noun in the accusative and the numeral agrees: jednu kávu (feminine).
- `er_rozumiem_no`: ✗ Ja no rozumiem. → **nerozumiem** / **ja nerozumiem**
  - explanation shown: Slovak negation is a prefix on the verb: NErozumiem — not a separate word like English „not“.

## Part B — Six recordings (~20 minutes)

We are benchmarking speech-recognition providers on real Slovak audio, and we
need known sentences from a native speaker. Phone microphone is perfect —
polished studio audio would actually be *wrong* for this test.

For each item: read the sentence **exactly as written**, once, in the listed
situation. Name the file by its ID (`u001.m4a`, `u004.m4a`, …). Any common
format is fine (m4a / wav / voice memo).

  > Dobrý deň, volám sa Ján Kováč.
- **u004** (inside a car or bus):
